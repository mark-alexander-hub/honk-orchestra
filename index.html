The street is the band.

Every horn, pressure-cooker whistle, bird or doorbell your phone hears gets tuned and dropped into a beat. One honk becomes a riff. A traffic jam becomes a song.

Hold the phone where the noise is. Earphones sound best; on the speaker it tries to ignore its own sounds.

The microphone is listened to live and nothing is uploaded. Only a recording you choose to make is saved, on your phone.
